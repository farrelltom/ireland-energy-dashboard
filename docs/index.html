More context

  • Wind was 29% of generation yesterday, up 19 percentage points vs last week.
  • Grid CO₂ intensity averaged 242 gCO₂/kWh yesterday.
  • System demand averaged 4020 MW yesterday (wind supplied roughly 1146 MW of that).
  • Ireland was a net importer of electricity yesterday, drawing an average of 1113 MW from interconnectors.
  • EV (home charging at €0.40/kWh) costs €7.20/100km vs €11.94/100km for petrol — 40% cheaper.
